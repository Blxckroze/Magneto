Privacy Policy for Magneto Last updated: March 22, 2026 Overview Magneto ("the app") is a digital product creation tool that helps you generate and publish products to Gumroad. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used. Data We Collect Magneto does not collect or store any personal data on external servers owned by the developer. The following information is stored locally on your device only: - Your Groq API key - Your Gumroad access token - Your backend server URL These are stored using Android's secure encrypted storage and are never transmitted to anyone other than the services you explicitly configure (Groq, Gumroad, your own server). Third-Party Services When you use the app, your data is sent to third-party services you have configured: - Groq (groq.com) — used to generate product plans and descriptions. Subject to Groq's privacy policy. - Gumroad (gumroad.com) — used to publish your digital products. Subject to Gumroad's privacy policy. - Your backend server — the Magneto server URL you provide processes image generation and PDF creation. - Pollinations.ai — used for AI image generation. No account or API key required. Data Sharing We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third party. We have no access to your API keys or any content you create. Data Retention All data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time through the Settings screen using "Clear All Keys," or by uninstalling the app. Children's Privacy This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
